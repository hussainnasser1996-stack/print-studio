The opportunity

Pharmaceutical and fresh-food imports into the Gulf are growing faster than the cold storage built to hold them. Most of today’s capacity was designed for frozen food, not for the narrow 2–8°C bands that biologics and vaccines require, and not for the audit trail that regulators now ask for.

Halcyon was built for exactly that gap. Its facilities are GDP-certified, every pallet is tracked by sensor from dock to door, and customers see temperature and location data in real time. Pharma distributors, hospital groups and premium grocers sign multi-year contracts because switching carries regulatory risk.

“Customers don’t leave a cold chain that has never broken. Our churn is a rounding error.”

Revenue grew {{G_FY24}}% in FY24 to US${{REV_FY24}} million, and the business turned EBITDA-positive in FY23. Utilisation across the network has been above 90% for six consecutive quarters: the constraint on growth is space, not demand.

Key risks and how they are managed

  • R1
    Customer concentration. The top five customers are 38% of revenue, down from 52% in FY22.
  • R2
    Construction delay. Fixed-price contracts with penalty clauses on all three new sites.
  • R3
    Energy cost. Rooftop solar covers 30% of load at the newest sites; power is passed through in most contracts.
